Data Deletion Instructions

According to Facebook/Meta Platform rules and general privacy laws, we provide a clear way for users to delete their account and the data we have collected.

If you wish to delete your NERO Player account and associated data from our servers, you have two options:

Option 1: Request Deletion via Email

You can request data deletion by sending an email to our support team.

  1. Send an email to support@neroapp.net.
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".
  3. In the body of the email, include the email address associated with your account.

Our team will process your request and permanently delete your account and associated data within 7 business days.

Option 2: Remove App Access via Facebook (If logged in via Meta)

If you use Facebook Login, you can remove NERO Player's access to your data directly through your Facebook account settings. This will stop any future data from being shared with us.

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click on Settings.
  3. Scroll down to Apps and Websites.
  4. Find Nero Player in the list of active apps.
  5. Click Remove.
  6. Check the box to delete posts, videos, or events NERO Player posted on your timeline (if any).
  7. Click Remove again to confirm.
Note: Removing the app from Facebook (Option 2) stops future data access but does not automatically delete the data we already hold (like your email address in our database). To ensure complete deletion of your data from our servers, please also email us as described in Option 1.
