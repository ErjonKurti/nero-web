Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to NERO Player ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website or use our applications and tell you about your privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following types of data:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, username or similar identifier.
  • Contact Data: Email address and telephone numbers.
  • Technical Data: IP address, login data, browser type and version, time zone setting, location, operating system, device IDs (e.g., Smart TV IDs, Android/iOS device IDs), and platform.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, app, and streaming services (e.g., watch history, favorites).

Information collected through Facebook/Meta Login

If you choose to register or log in using your Facebook or Instagram account, we request access to the following basic profile information provided by Meta:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • Your Profile Picture

We do not request access to your friends list, timeline posts, or any other sensitive data from your social media accounts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our streaming platform.
  • Process your registration and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your login across multiple devices (Mobile, TV, Web).
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., remembering your watch history and favorites).
  • Communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions, monitor against theft, and protect against criminal activity.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, cloud hosting providers, and customer support tools.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered, or disclosed. Your passwords are encrypted, and we use secure protocols (HTTPS) for data transmission.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. If you logged in via Facebook, you can manage your permissions directly from your Facebook account settings.

To request complete deletion of your data from our servers, please visit our Data Deletion Instructions page or contact us directly.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may email us at support@neroapp.net.
